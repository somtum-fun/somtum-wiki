# Old osu! clients (2008-2015)

somtum.fun lets you play osu! the way it was: we rebuilt **75 osu! clients from 2008 to 2015** to connect to our server. Play the version you started with, and your scores still share one leaderboard with today's osu!(stable) and lazer (TumThai).

## Getting one {id=getting-one}

1. Pick a build on the [download page](/home/download), or let the **Somtum Launcher** install it for you.
2. Unzip it anywhere and run `osu!.exe`.
3. Keep `pkms.dll`, our anti-cheat, next to `osu!.exe`. Without it, your scores aren't submitted.

The old clients need Windows and .NET Framework 3.5 (a few 2015 builds need .NET Framework 4).

## Only our builds can log in {id=only-our-builds}

Each client is our own patched build of that version. An unmodified old build, one with the old hook, or one you rebuilt yourself can't connect: see ["This old osu! build can't play on somtum.fun as it is"](/wiki/FAQ#error-old-build).

Can't find the version you want? Ask in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/U6Ztb3mRyz) or on the [forum](/community/forums/4).
