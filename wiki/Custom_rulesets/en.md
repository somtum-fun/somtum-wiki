# Custom rulesets

Besides osu!, osu!taiko, osu!catch and osu!mania, you can play **custom rulesets** on somtum.fun: game modes made by the community for osu!lazer, from [rulesets.info](https://rulesets.info/). Each one has its own leaderboards, rankings and profile tab here, ranked **by score**.

## Supported rulesets {id=supported}

These are the rulesets somtum.fun ranks right now. Staff add more over time; the list in TumThai (see [below](#install)) is always the current one.

| Ruleset | What it is | Made by |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| [sentakki](https://github.com/LumpBloom7/sentakki) | Tap, hold and slide to the beat, inspired by maimai | LumpBloom7 |
| [Tau](https://github.com/taulazer/tau) | Sweep the beats with your paddle around a circle | taulazer |
| [Rush!](https://github.com/Beamographic/rush) | Loosely based on Muse Dash | Beamographic |

| [FPSosu!](https://github.com/theokrueger/FPSosu) | First-person aim training | theokrueger |
| [Swing](https://github.com/EVAST9919/lazer-swing) | A rhythm mode of its own | EVAST9919 |

Each ruleset belongs to its developers. Report a bug in the gameplay itself on that ruleset's page; report a problem with scores or leaderboards here to us.

## Installing a ruleset {id=install}

Custom rulesets work in **TumThai** (our osu!lazer client) on Windows, Linux and macOS, and in **TumThai!Next** on Android too (pick TumThai!Next on the [download page](/home/download?stream=next)). They don't work in osu!(stable), the old clients or TumThai's Android app.

On a phone, pick a ruleset made for touch, such as **sentakki**. Many of the others (osu!fps, FPSosu!, Tau, bosu, touhosu) need a mouse and keyboard.

1. Sign in to TumThai.
2. Open **Settings → General → Custom rulesets** and press **Install custom rulesets…**
3. Press **Install** next to a ruleset. It appears in the ruleset bar at the top of the screen right away.

The same window shows when a ruleset has an **update**: press **Update**, then restart TumThai. Removing a ruleset also takes effect when TumThai restarts.

Only the version somtum.fun ranks submits scores, so install and update rulesets from this window. A ruleset file you put in TumThai's `rulesets` folder yourself still plays, but its scores only submit when it's the same file. If a ruleset says *Doesn't work with this TumThai version*, it needs an update from its developers; until then it can't be played.

## Scores and rankings {id=scores}

- **Ranked by score.** Custom rulesets give no performance points (pp), so there's no pp rank for them.
- **Which plays count:** a passed play on an osu! map with a leaderboard (ranked, approved, qualified or loved). Plays with Autoplay or Cinema, or a ruleset's own auto-play mod, don't count.
- **Leaderboards:** each map has a leaderboard per ruleset, with your best score on it. Relax and Autopilot plays have their own boards, as in osu!.
- **Rankings:** the [custom ruleset rankings](/rankings/rulesets) add up everyone's best score on each ranked map (ranked score).
- **Your profile** has a tab for each ruleset you've played, with your rank, scores and rank history.
- **Star ratings** show for rulesets that calculate them (Tau and soyokaze, for example).

Your new score shows on the website and in the Discord bot as soon as it's submitted. TumThai 2026.1026.1 and newer show your new rank on the results screen too.

## What isn't supported {id=not-supported}

- **Performance points**: rankings are by score only.
- **Multiplayer and spectating.**
- **Replay renders**: the score page and the Discord bot say a render isn't available for a custom ruleset.
- **Medals.**
- **osu!(stable), the old clients and TumThai's Android app** (TumThai!Next on Android works).
