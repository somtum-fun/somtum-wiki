# Upgrading to lazer

On somtum.fun, lazer is **TumThai**: our version of osu!(lazer), set up to play here. You can use it next to
osu!(stable); both use the same somtum.fun account.

## Getting TumThai {id=getting-tumthai}

- **Windows:** download it from the [download page](/home/download), or use
  [Somtum Launcher](/home/download), which installs and starts it for you.
- **Linux:** the AppImage on the [download page](/home/download).
- **Android:** the APK on the [download page](/home/download).

TumThai keeps itself up to date: when there's a new version, it tells you and updates when you restart it.

## Bringing your stable things over {id=import-from-stable}

TumThai can copy your beatmaps, skins, scores and collections from osu!(stable):

1. Open **Settings → General → Run setup wizard**, or **Settings → Maintenance**.
2. Choose **Import from osu!(stable)** and point it at your osu! folder.

Nothing is changed or removed in your stable folder.

## What's different {id=differences}

lazer plays and scores a little differently from stable (for example, accuracy, combo and some mods). osu!'s
wiki explains it in detail:

- [Upgrading to osu!(lazer)](https://osu.ppy.sh/wiki/Help_centre/Upgrading_to_lazer)
- [Gameplay differences in osu!(lazer)](https://osu.ppy.sh/wiki/Client/Release_stream/Lazer/Gameplay_differences_in_osu!(lazer))

What's different on somtum.fun itself:

- You sign in with your **somtum.fun** account, not your osu! one.
- Your profile, rankings and beatmap leaderboards are on [our website](/home).
- Multiplayer, spectating and chat are with other somtum.fun players.
