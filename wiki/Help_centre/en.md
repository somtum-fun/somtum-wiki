# Help centre

Something wrong? Here's where to go.

## Quick answers

Most questions are answered in the [FAQ](/wiki/FAQ): connecting, accounts, scores that didn't submit, and more.

## Contact us

| For | Where |
| :-- | :-- |
| General help and bugs | Our [Discord](https://discord.gg/U6Ztb3mRyz), or [support@somtum.fun](mailto:support@somtum.fun) |
| Your account (sign-up problems, username changes, a lost account) | [accounts@somtum.fun](mailto:accounts@somtum.fun) |
| Restrictions and appeals | Sign in and use the [appeal page](/home/appeal); see [Restrictions & appeals](/legal/en/Restrictions) |
| Copyright (DMCA) | [dmca@somtum.fun](mailto:dmca@somtum.fun); see [Copyright](/legal/en/Copyright) |
| Harassment and abuse | See [Reporting abuse](/wiki/Reporting_bad_behaviour/Abuse) |

Email us from your account's email address when you can, so we know it's you. We answer by email, usually within a few days.

## Reporting a player

Use the report button in the game or on the player's profile. Staff see every report.
