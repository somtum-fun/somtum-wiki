# Reporting abuse

## I don't know if I should report this or not {id=reportworthiness}

Please do. If something someone did makes you uncomfortable enough to read this page, tell us. There's no time limit: report it even if it happened a while ago.

We don't tolerate harassment, threats, hate speech, or any unwanted sexual attention or advances.

## How to report {id=how-to-report}

### In the game chat {id=chat}

Block the player (right-click their name: `Ignore User` in osu!(stable), `Block` in TumThai). Then report them with `Report User...` or `Report`, and say what happened.

### On the website {id=website}

Open the player's profile, click the menu next to their name and choose `Report`. Forum posts, comments and beatmap discussions have their own report button.

### Anywhere else, or if it's serious {id=email}

Email [support@somtum.fun](mailto:support@somtum.fun) with as much detail as you can: who, when, where, and screenshots if you have them. Only staff read it.

If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

## What happens next {id=what-happens-next}

Staff look at every report. We may not tell you what we did, to protect everyone's privacy, but we read them all. Making a false report on purpose is against the [Rules](/legal/en/Rules).
