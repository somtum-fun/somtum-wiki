# Bot account

A bot account lets a program chat on somtum.fun, for example to run a tournament, answer commands, or post announcements.

## What a bot can do {id=what-a-bot-can-do}

- Chat over IRC: server `irc.blueskychan.dev`, port `6667`, using the bot's name and its IRC password.
- Use the website and the [API](/wiki/API), for example to manage a team or post on the forums.

A bot can't log in to the game (TumThai or osu!(stable)), and it has no scores. Website verification codes for a bot go to its owner.

## How to get one {id=how-to-get-one}

Bot accounts are made by our administrators and developers, and each one has an owner who is responsible for it. Ask on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/U6Ztb3mRyz) or email [support@somtum.fun](mailto:support@somtum.fun), and tell us:

- the bot's name,
- what it will do,
- which account will own it.

Please don't turn your own player account into a bot. Bots must follow the [Rules](/legal/en/Rules), and their owner answers for them.
