# Rules

The full community rules are on the [Rules](/legal/en/Rules) page. By playing on somtum.fun, you agree to them. This page adds the rules for submitting beatmaps.

## Beatmap submission rules {id=beatmap-submission-rules}

- Submit maps from the editor in TumThai or osu!(stable). Only the set's owner can update it, and nobody can once it's qualified, ranked or loved.
- You can't submit while your account is restricted or silenced.
- A set has 1 to 128 difficulties, all with the same artist and title.
- You can have 4 sets in progress, plus 1 more for each of your ranked sets (up to 8). Supporters get 8, up to 20. Sets untouched for four weeks don't count.
- Don't submit maps that aren't yours, or someone else's map under your name.
- Keep it safe for work: no NSFW or offensive backgrounds, videos, storyboards or titles. See [Visual content considerations](/wiki/Rules/Visual_content_considerations).
- Don't submit content you don't have the right to share. Copyright holders can ask us to take content down at [dmca@somtum.fun](mailto:dmca@somtum.fun).

To get a map ranked, see the [ranking procedure](/wiki/Beatmap_ranking_procedure). For how to map well, the [osu! ranking criteria](https://osu.ppy.sh/wiki/Ranking_criteria) are a good guide, and our Beatmap Nominators use them too.
