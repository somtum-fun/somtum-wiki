# Visual content considerations

What's fine for avatars, profile covers and pages, signatures, and beatmap backgrounds, videos and storyboards on somtum.fun.

## Not allowed {id=not-allowed}

- Nudity, sexual content or anything suggestive.
- Gore, graphic violence or shocking images.
- Hate symbols, slurs, or anything that attacks people for who they are.
- Flashing images or jumpscares that could hurt someone.
- Images of real people shared without their consent, or someone's personal information.
- Impersonating other players or staff.

## Use common sense {id=common-sense}

Players of every age use somtum.fun. If you're not sure whether something is fine, it probably isn't. Staff can remove content and reset avatars or covers that break these rules, and repeat breaks can lead to a silence or restriction (see the [Rules](/legal/en/Rules)).
