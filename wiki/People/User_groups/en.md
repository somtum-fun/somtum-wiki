# User groups

**User groups** show what a player does on somtum.fun. A group shows as a short badge next to the player's name (such as **GMT** or **BN**), and the player's name and [title](/wiki/People/User_title) take the colour of their main group. Click a badge to see everyone in that group.

## Team groups {id=team}

These are the people who run somtum.fun. What each team does is on [the team page](/wiki/People/Team), and changes to them are listed openly in the [staff log](/wiki/People/Staff_log).

| Badge | Group | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| ADM | [Administrators](/groups/1) | Run the server and the website. |
| DEV | [Developers](/groups/8) | Build TumThai, the game server, the website and our tools. |
| GMT | [Global Moderation Team](/groups/9) | Moderate chat, forums and profiles, and look at reports. |
| SPT | [Support Team](/groups/14) | Help players with their accounts and problems. |
| NAT | [Nomination Assessment Team](/groups/11) | Look after the Beatmap Nominators and ranking quality. |
| BN | [Beatmap Nominators](/groups/4) and [probationary BNs](/groups/5) | Nominate maps for ranking. |
| LVD | [Project Loved](/groups/10) | Choose maps for the Loved category. |
| TC | [Tournament Committee](/groups/15) | Run our tournaments. |
| MDL | [Medal Team](/groups/17) | Review the [custom medals](/community/medals) players ask for. |
| ALM | [Alumni](/groups/2) | Former staff, thanked for their work. |

Some groups (BN, NAT, Project Loved) show the game modes they work in as small mode icons next to the badge.

| Badge | Group | |

**Supporter** isn't a group: supporters get the heart on their profile instead.

## Joining a group {id=joining}

You can't add yourself to a group. Staff add people:

- **Beatmap Nominators** apply through [BN Management](https://bn.blueskychan.dev) when applications are open; the NAT decide. See the [ranking procedure](/wiki/Beatmap_ranking_procedure).
- **Other teams** ask for help when they need it, on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/U6Ztb3mRyz). Being friendly and helpful in the community is the best way to be noticed.
- **Chat Bots:** see [Bot account](/wiki/Bot_account).

A group gives its members tools on the website and in the game for that job only. Staff will never ask for your password.
