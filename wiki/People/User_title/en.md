# User titles

A **user title** is the line of text under a player's name on their profile, in the forum and on their player card, such as *Tournament Committee* or *Former somtum.fun Champion*. A player can have **two titles**. The second one is shown after the first: *Tournament Committee / Mapping Architect*.

A title takes the colour of the player's main [user group](/wiki/People/User_groups), and it can link somewhere, such as a tournament's page.

## Getting a title {id=getting-one}

Staff give titles. You can't pick one yourself, and they aren't for sale or included with supporter tags. You might get one:

- **with a team role:** the Tournament Committee, the Medal Team and Featured Artists get their group's title when they join the group, and lose it when they leave;
- **for something you did:** winning or running a tournament, helping the server in a big way, a community event;
- **just for fun,** now and then, from staff.

If you think you've earned one, ask on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/U6Ztb3mRyz). Please don't message staff privately to ask.

Players who hold a one-of-a-kind title are listed on [Users with unique titles](/wiki/People/Users_with_unique_titles).

## Losing a title {id=losing-one}

A title from a team role goes when you leave the group. Staff can also take a title away, for example if it was given by mistake or if it's used to pretend to be staff. A title isn't a staff role: holding one gives you no extra rights.
