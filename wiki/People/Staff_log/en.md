# Staff log

The group history on the website shows when players join or leave a [team group](/wiki/People/Team), and who made the change. Everyone can see it, so changes to the team are open.

Other staff actions, such as restrictions, silences and score removals, are kept in a private log that only staff can see. If one of those affects your account, you'll hear from us by email or in the game. See [Restrictions & appeals](/legal/en/Restrictions).
