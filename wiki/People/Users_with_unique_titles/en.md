## PP Breaker {id=pp-breaker}

In November 2026, these four players found that a lazer play with DT set to a custom speed, like 1.01×, was given pp as if it was played at 1.5×. Thanks to them, the [custom speed pp glitch](/wiki/Performance_points/Custom_speed_glitch) got found and fixed, and every play is now paid at the speed it was played.
