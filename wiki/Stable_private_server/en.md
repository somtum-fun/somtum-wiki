# Connecting osu!(stable) to a private server

You can play osu!(stable) on somtum.fun, a free osu! private server open to players worldwide, with your own osu! install. Your stable scores share one leaderboard with lazer (TumThai) and the old 2008-2015 clients.

## The easy way: the Somtum Launcher {id=launcher}

Download the **Somtum Launcher** from the [download page](/home/download) (Windows and Linux). It starts osu!(stable) on somtum.fun for you, with our anti-cheat, so your scores are submitted. It also installs and updates TumThai and the old clients.

## The Somtum Patcher {id=patcher}

Prefer not to use the launcher? Start osu!(stable) with the **Somtum Patcher** from the [download page](/home/download). It connects your normal osu! to somtum.fun and runs our anti-cheat, so your scores are submitted. It also adds a live pp counter.

## Make your account {id=account}

Make your account in the game, not on the website: start osu! on somtum.fun, register there, then sign in to this website with the same name and password.

## What about `-devserver`? {id=devserver}

Starting osu! with `-devserver somtum.fun` connects too, and you can chat, play and join multiplayer, but **your scores aren't submitted**: only the Somtum Launcher and the Somtum Patcher run the anti-cheat that lets them count. See [How do I connect?](/wiki/FAQ#how-do-i-connect)

## Going back to osu! {id=going-back}

Start osu! normally, without the launcher or the patcher, and it connects to osu.ppy.sh as before.
