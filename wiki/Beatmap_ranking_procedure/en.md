# Beatmap ranking procedure

How a map submitted to somtum.fun becomes ranked. The steps are like osu!'s, but faster.

## 1. Submit {id=submit}

Submit your map from the editor in TumThai or osu!(stable) (see the [submission rules](/wiki/Rules#beatmap-submission-rules)). Mark it **finished** to make it **pending**; a work in progress isn't looked at.

A map from osu! that's in the graveyard there can also be opened for nominations by our Beatmap Nominators.

## 2. Get feedback and hype {id=hype}

Post your map's discussion page link on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/U6Ztb3mRyz) and ask for mods. Players can **hype** a pending map (each player has 3 hypes a week). A map needs **1 hype** before it can be nominated.

## 3. Nominations {id=nominations}

Two [Beatmap Nominators](/groups/bng) need to nominate the map on its discussion page. They check it against the [osu! ranking criteria](https://osu.ppy.sh/wiki/Ranking_criteria). With 2 nominations, the map is **qualified**.

## 4. Qualified and ranked {id=qualified}

A qualified map is ranked by itself after **1 day**, as long as nobody finds a problem. If someone does, a nominator or the [Nomination Assessment Team](/groups/nat) can disqualify it, and it goes back to pending until it's fixed and nominated again.

See the [ranking queue](/wiki/Beatmap_ranking_procedure/Ranking_queue) for what's about to be ranked.
