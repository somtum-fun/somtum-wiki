# Tournaments

Running a tournament on somtum.fun? Matches are played in multiplayer like on osu!, and our staff can help with
referees and setup. Ask on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/U6Ztb3mRyz).

- [Tournament client](/wiki/Tournaments/Tournament_client): showing a match on stream, with TumThai or osu!(stable)

For how tournaments work in general (brackets, mappools, referee commands), see
[osu!'s tournament pages](https://osu.ppy.sh/wiki/Tournaments).
