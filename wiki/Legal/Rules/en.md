# Rules

By playing on somtum.fun, you agree to follow these rules. Breaking them can lead to anything from a warning to a permanent ban.

## 1. General rules

1. **Use common sense.** Respect others, don't look for loopholes, and keep things fair and fun for everyone.
2. **No cheating, hacking or botting.** Any cheat, client modification or third-party tool that gives you an unfair advantage is not allowed.
3. **One account per person.** If you need a new account because of a problem, contact us at [accounts@somtum.fun](mailto:accounts@somtum.fun) first.
4. **Make your account in the game, without a VPN or proxy.** Accounts are made in TumThai or osu! stable, not on the website. Sign-ups through a VPN, proxy, Tor or hosting network are blocked, and each network (a home, school or phone) can make one account a day. Don't try to get around this; if it stops you from signing up honestly, email [accounts@somtum.fun](mailto:accounts@somtum.fun).
5. **No account sharing.** Your account represents you. Don't share it, and don't play on someone else's.
6. **Report rule breakers.** If you see someone breaking these rules, report them in the game or from their profile (or on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/U6Ztb3mRyz)). Helping rule breakers can get you punished too.
7. **No bug exploiting.** If you find a bug, tell a developer instead of using it. Responsible reports may even be rewarded.

## 2. Community behaviour

1. **Be respectful; no harassment.** Treat everyone with kindness. Harassment, discrimination and toxic behaviour aren't tolerated.
2. **No hate speech.** Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia or any other bigotry, including slurs, is strictly forbidden.
3. **No drama or public hackusations.** If you have a problem with someone, report it privately. Public complaints about bans and accusations against other players aren't allowed.
4. **English in public chats.** Public channels are English only. Other languages are fine in private messages and in channels made for them.
5. **No spamming or flooding.** Avoid sending lots of messages, unnecessary links or anything else disruptive.

## 3. Content and communication

1. **Keep it safe for work.** No NSFW or offensive content, and nothing that could hurt someone (such as flashing images or jumpscares).
2. **No advertising or unwanted links.** Don't promote other servers, pirated software or unrelated content.
3. **No talk about cheating or illegal activities.** Encouraging cheating or discussing anything illegal isn't allowed.

## 4. Moderation

1. **Follow staff instructions.** If a staff member asks you to do something, do it.
2. **Don't impersonate staff.** Pretending to be a staff member is a serious offense.
3. **Don't abuse support.** Repeated pointless appeals, name change requests or disruptive behaviour in support channels will get you restricted.

## 5. Appeals and punishments

1. **How to report a player.** Use the report button in the game or on their profile, or email [support@somtum.fun](mailto:support@somtum.fun).
2. **How to appeal.** If you think your punishment was unfair, you can appeal once your appeal cooldown is over. Every appeal is looked at case by case. See [Restrictions & Appeals](/legal/en/Restrictions) for how it works.
3. **How bad it gets.** Depending on how serious it was, punishments include warnings, chat silences, restrictions, and temporary or permanent bans.

## 6. Final notes

- **These rules can change.** Staff may update them at any time.
- **Use common sense.** If something feels wrong, it probably is.
- **Serious offenses can end your account.** Severe violations, such as trying to damage the server, may lead to a permanent ban with no chance to appeal.

**By continuing to use our services, you agree to these rules.**
