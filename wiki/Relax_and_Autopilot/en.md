# Relax and Autopilot

On somtum.fun, a free osu! private server, **Relax and Autopilot** have their own pp, leaderboards and rankings, in both lazer (TumThai) and osu!(stable).

## How it works {id=how-it-works}

- **Relax** taps for you, so it's about aim and accuracy. It works in osu!, osu!taiko and osu!catch.
- **Autopilot** moves the cursor for you, so it's about tapping and accuracy. It works in osu! only.
- Turn the mod on and play: your play goes to the Relax or Autopilot leaderboard of that map, not the normal one.

## Rankings {id=rankings}

Choose **Relax** or **Autopilot** on the [rankings page](/rankings/osu/global) and on beatmap leaderboards. Top plays have their own lists too: [Relax](/rankings/top-plays/osu?variant=rx) and [Autopilot](/rankings/top-plays/osu?variant=ap).

## pp {id=pp}

Relax and Autopilot pp comes from [akatsuki-pp-rs](https://github.com/osuAkatsuki/akatsuki-pp-rs), calculators made for these mods, so it isn't comparable with normal pp. See [Performance points](/wiki/Performance_points) for each mode's calculator.
