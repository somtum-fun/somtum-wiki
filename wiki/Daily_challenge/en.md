# Daily challenge

The **daily challenge** is osu!lazer's own daily event, and it works the same way on somtum.fun. Every day a new map opens in a playlist room in **TumThai**: play it as often as you like, and your best score counts. Open it from the main menu: **Play → Daily challenge**.

## How it works {id=how-it-works}

- A new challenge opens every day at **00:05 UTC** and runs until the next one opens. TumThai tells you when a new one is up.
- Only plays made **inside the daily challenge room** count. Playing the same map from song select doesn't.
- The room's leaderboard ranks players by their **best total score**. You see new scores from other players as they come in.
- After each day, your **streak** (days in a row you played) and how well you placed (your percentile) are worked out. They show on your profile and in the [daily challenge ranking](/rankings/daily-challenge).
- Our Discord bot posts each day's challenge in our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/U6Ztb3mRyz), then the top five when it ends.

The daily challenge is **TumThai (osu!lazer) only**: osu!(stable) has no daily challenge.

## The maps {id=maps}

Every map is picked automatically from **ranked** maps that haven't been a daily challenge before. Each one is 1 to 5 minutes long. Like on osu!, the week starts easy and gets harder:

| Day | Star rating |
| :-- | :-- |
| Thursday | 3 to 3.5★ |
| Friday | 3.5 to 4★ |
| Saturday | 4 to 4.5★ |
| Sunday | 4.5 to 5★ |
| Monday | 5 to 5.5★ |
| Tuesday | 5.5 to 6★ |
| Wednesday | 6 to 6.5★ |

The challenges are in **osu!** mode for now.

## Mods {id=mods}

You can add these mods to your plays, but nothing that changes the speed (no Double Time, Half Time and the like):

| Mode | Allowed mods |
| :-- | :-- |
| osu!, osu!taiko, osu!catch | Easy, No Fail, Hidden, Hard Rock, Sudden Death, Perfect, Flashlight |
| osu!mania | Easy, No Fail, Hidden, Fade In, Sudden Death, Perfect, Flashlight, Mirror |

## Daily challenge or map of the day? {id=map-of-the-day}

somtum.fun also has a [map of the day](/wiki/Map_of_the_day) on Discord. They're two different things:

| | Daily challenge | Map of the day |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Where | A room in TumThai | Any normal play on the map, in TumThai or osu!(stable) |
| Map | Ranked, 1–5 minutes, harder each day of the week | Random ranked map, 3–6.5★, 1–7 minutes |
| Mods | Only the ones above | Any (except Relax and Autopilot) |
| Who wins | Best total score in the room | Best pass that day by pp, then score |
| Reward | Streaks, percentile and the daily challenge ranking | The Map of the Day Discord role for a week |
